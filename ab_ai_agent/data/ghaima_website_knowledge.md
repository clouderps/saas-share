Curated from https://ghaima.sa. Reference material only.

## Product
Ghaima (غيمة) is a cloud ERP platform for Saudi businesses: Point of Sale, HR & Payroll, Accounting, Inventory and Multi-Branch management in one platform. ZATCA compliant, full Arabic/RTL, cloud hosted. Arabic and English work side by side across backend, POS, website and reports.

## Modules
- Point of Sale: touch interface, multiple sessions, Kitchen Display System (real-time, multiple stations, audio alerts), customer QR self-registration with OTP, laundry mode, role-based access, Mobile POS app.
- Sales & Inventory: quotations, sales orders, purchases, warehouse transfers, multi-location stock.
- HR & Payroll: 35+ modules from recruitment to end of service; contracts, org chart, attendance and shifts, leaves, loans, GOSI, WPS, End of Service per Saudi Labour Law.
- Accounting: general ledger, invoicing, payments, assets, 15+ financial reports, ZATCA e-invoicing.
- Multi-Branch: each branch keeps its own sales, inventory and POS data in one instance, with central reporting.
- Dashboards & AI: customizable dashboards, AI reporting in natural language, trend analysis, anomaly detection, forecasting.
- Also: Manufacturing (MRP), CRM & Loyalty, multi-company (Professional plan).

## Plans
Billed monthly or annually (annual is 15% off). Upgrade any time and pay only the prorated difference.
- Basic: POS, Kitchen Display, Mobile POS app, basic dashboard, ZATCA, daily backups (30-day retention), AI tokens included.
- Advanced (most popular): Basic + Sales & Purchase, Inventory & Warehouse, Multi-Branch isolation, advanced dashboards, employee management, 90-day backup retention.
- Professional: Advanced + full Accounting, HR & Payroll, Manufacturing, CRM & Loyalty, multi-company, priority support, 180-day backup retention.

## Hosting and security
Each customer gets an isolated instance (own database and secure HTTPS subdomain), automated backups, encrypted credentials, 99.9% uptime target with monitoring. Data is kept 30 days after cancellation; to cancel, let the billing cycle end.
